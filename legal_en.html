Lessons, word lists, example sentences and audio all work offline. A network connection is used only if you buy or restore a purchase, which is handled by the App Store. The app contains no remote content, no remote configuration and no web view.

5. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+. Because we collect no data at all, we do not knowingly collect information from children — there is nothing to collect.

6. Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. You can erase all local data at any time in the app (Me → Reset progress) or by deleting the app. This satisfies the access, erasure and portability rights under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA and similar laws.

7. Data retention and security

Learning data stays on your device until you delete it. It is protected by iOS’s own app sandbox and device encryption. We do not maintain backups of it.

If a future version adds a feature that sends data off the device, this policy will be updated before that version ships and the change will be described in the release notes.
