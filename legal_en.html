Privacy Policy

1. Local learning data

Progress, assessment results, saved words, personal words and settings are stored on this device. Reset progress removes learning records; personal words can be deleted in My Words. Deleting the app removes its local data.

2. Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking practice asks for microphone permission only when you record. Recordings are temporary and deleted when you leave the practice screen. Optional speech recognition is requested only on supported devices and requires on-device recognition. Recordings stay on the device. Text agreement is a practice aid, not a professional pronunciation score.

3. Backup and sharing

You can export progress and personal words to a JSON file through Backup and restore. The file is saved to the destination you choose, which may be a cloud file provider. Keep it private. Progress-card sharing includes only the totals shown on the card. We do not keep your backups.

4. Network and third parties

Courses, listening, assessments, the widget and the study guide work offline. Apple handles purchases and restoration. Optional system translation may download language resources. No advertising or analytics SDK is included.

5. Contact and changes

For support or privacy questions, contact 2603462296@qq.com. Review this page when updating the app. Local files you export are managed by you and your chosen storage provider.
