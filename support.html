Learning tools and backups

Open Learn → Practice for a study plan, listening playlists, spelling, word matching, speaking practice, placement checks and level tests. Me → Learning statistics provides 7/14/30/90-day views and progress sharing. A daily-word widget is also included.

Me → Settings → Backup and restore exports your progress and personal words to a private JSON file. Import validates the file and asks before replacement. Keep your backup private. Resetting progress does not delete your personal wordbook; delete those words separately in My Words.

Microphone access is needed only for recording. Recordings are temporary and deleted when you leave; offline text recognition is not a pronunciation grade. Recordings stay on the device.

About the app

Hangul & Words is a self-contained Korean course for beginners. It teaches the alphabet (Hangul), how syllables are assembled, the sound-change rules, 296 course words across 13 topics and 5,000 everyday expressions, with explanations in English, Japanese and Traditional Chinese.

  • Works offline. All lessons and pronunciation audio are built in — nothing is downloaded or streamed.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing uploaded.
  • No ads, no tracking. No advertising SDK and no analytics of any kind.
  • Three interface languages. English, 日本語 and 繁體中文 — switchable at any time in Profile → Language.
  • Spaced repetition. Every word you meet comes back at the right moment (1, 2, 4, 7, 15, 30 days).

Frequently asked questions

I paid before — how do I get Pro back?

Open Me → Settings → Restore purchases and sign in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase. Restoration is instant and free. If nothing happens, make sure the device is online and that the Apple ID region matches the store you bought from.

There is no sound.

Bundled pronunciation clips play offline. New course words and phrases, and syllables you build yourself, use the device’s Korean system voice.

  • Turn the ringer/silent switch off silent, and raise the volume with the side buttons.
  • Settings → Sounds & Haptics — make sure media volume is not muted.
  • Disconnect Bluetooth headphones; audio may be routed to a device you are not wearing.
  • If only new course entries or the syllable builder are silent, install a Korean voice in the device’s accessibility settings.
What is free and what needs Pro?

Free study includes all 40 letters, sound rules, the syllable builder, two word themes, the greetings and dining requests sets, and 200 graded words. One Pro purchase unlocks every course, word and phrase. The app displays your local price before you confirm.

Can I use the app without buying anything?

Yes. The free part is a complete introduction to reading Hangul and is not time-limited. There is no trial countdown on it and no nag screen before a lesson.

How do I change the interface language?

Profile → Language, then pick English, 日本語 or 中文. The change is immediate — no restart. The app also follows your system language the first time it launches.

Is my progress backed up?

Progress lives in the app's own storage on this device and is included in a normal iCloud or Finder device backup. We run no server, so we cannot restore it for you. If you delete the app, the progress stored with it is deleted too.

How do I erase everything?

Profile → Reset all progress. This clears lessons, XP, streak, saved words and settings on this device. It does not affect a Pro purchase — that stays with your Apple ID.

Where does the content come from?

Every word, example sentence, phrase and grammar note was written for this app. We do not copy dictionary entries, textbook exercises, subtitles or lyrics. Icons are Apple SF Symbols and the app icon was drawn by us. Pronunciation audio is machine-synthesised speech, generated with a neural voice model and bundled with the app — it is not recorded from any person, so no voice rights are involved. See the content & copyright notice.

I found a mistake, or a word I want added.

Please email us with the screen name and, if you can, the word or sentence. Correction reports are read first, and content updates ship with regular app updates.

よくある質問

以前購入した Pro を復元したい。

マイページ → 設定 → 購入を復元 を開き、購入時と同じ Apple ID でサインインしてください。 復元は無料です。反応がない場合は、通信環境と、購入したストアと Apple ID の地域が一致しているかを ご確認ください。

音が出ません。

同梱の発音音声はオフラインで再生できます。追加の単語やフレーズ、組み立てた音節は端末の韓国語音声で読み上げます。

  • 本体側面の消音スイッチを解除し、音量を上げる。
  • 設定 → サウンドと触覚 でメディア音量がミュートになっていないか確認する。
  • Bluetooth イヤホンを切断する（見えない機器に音声が送られている場合があります）。
  • 追加の学習内容や文字の組み立て機能だけ音が出ない場合は、端末のアクセシビリティ設定で韓国語の音声をダウンロードしてください。
無料で使える範囲と Pro の違いは？

ハングル40文字、発音ルール、音節ビルダー、単語の最初の2テーマ、あいさつと注文・食事のフレーズ集、レベル別単語200語は無料です。Proを一度購入すると、すべてのコース・単語・フレーズを利用できます。価格は購入前に表示されます。

購入せずに使えますか？

使えます。無料部分はハングルを読むための入門として完結しており、期限もありません。 期間のカウントダウンや、レッスン前の購入催促もありません。

表示言語を変えるには？

プロフィール → 言語 から日本語・英語・中国語を選べます。再起動は不要で、 その場で切り替わります。初回起動時は端末の言語に合わせて表示されます。

常見問題

之前買過 Pro，怎麼恢復？

打開「我的 → 設定 → 還原購買」，用當初購買時的同一個 Apple ID 登錄即可，免費且即時生效。 如果沒有反應，請確認網絡正常，並檢查 Apple ID 所在地區與購買的商店是否一致。

沒有聲音。

內建的發音音訊可離線播放。新增詞句和拼字器組合出的音節使用裝置的韓語系統語音。

  • 關閉機身側面的靜音開關，並用音量鍵調大音量。
  • 設置 → 聲音與觸感 中確認媒體音量未被靜音。
  • 斷開藍牙耳機（音頻可能被送到未佩戴的設備）。
  • 若只有新增詞句或音節拼裝器沒有聲音，請在裝置的輔助使用設定中下載韓語語音。
免費範圍和 Pro 有什麼區別？

免費內容包含 40 個韓文字母、音變規則、音節拼裝器、前兩個主題詞表、問候與點餐需求兩組常用句，以及 200 個分級詞。購買 Pro 可一次解鎖全部課程、單詞與常用句。確認購買前會顯示當地價格。

不買也能用嗎？

可以。免費部分是完整的韓文認讀入門，沒有時間限制，也沒有試用倒計時或課前彈窗。

怎麼改界面語言？

「我的 → 語言」，選擇中文、日文或英文，立即生效，無需重啓。首次啓動時會跟隨系統語言。

學習記錄會備份嗎？

記錄保存在本機 App 的存儲中，會包含在 iCloud 或 Finder 的設備備份里。我們沒有服務器， 無法代為恢復。刪除 App 時，隨 App 保存的記錄也會被刪除。

怎麼清空全部數據？

「我的 → 重置學習記錄」，會清除本機的課程進度、XP、連擊、收藏單詞與設置。 不影響 Pro 購買，購買權益與 Apple ID 綁定。

內容從哪來？

所有單詞、例句、短語與語法說明均為本 App 專門撰寫，未轉載詞典釋義、教材練習、字幕或歌詞。 圖標使用 Apple SF Symbols，App 圖標為自繪。發音音頻是神經網絡語音合成的合成音，隨 App 打包， 不是真人錄音，因此不涉及聲音權或錄音版權。詳見內容與版權聲明。

發現錯誤，或希望增加某個詞。

請發郵件告訴我們所在的頁面，並盡量附上出錯的單詞或句子。糾錯反饋會優先處理， 內容更新隨常規版本發佈。
